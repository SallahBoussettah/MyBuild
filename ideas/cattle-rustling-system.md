# Cattle Rustling & Ranch Operations

## Overview
Players can steal, transport, and sell cattle from NPC and player-owned ranches. Ranch owners can hire guards, set up defenses, and track stolen livestock. This system integrates with the economy and reputation mechanics.

## System Workflow

### 1. Ranch Ownership
- Players can purchase/maintain ranches of varying sizes
- Livestock breeding and health management system
- Ranch staff hiring and management (cowboys, guards)
- Property improvements that affect productivity
- Seasonal events affecting livestock value and health

### 2. Rustling Preparation
- Intelligence gathering on ranch security and routines
- Equipment requirements (lasso, cattle prod, wagon)
- Time-of-day affects success probability (night operations easier)
- Posse formation with role assignment
- Bribery option for inside information

### 3. Cattle Theft Execution
- Stealth mechanics when approaching ranch
- Livestock handling skill affects animal behavior
- Noise management system to avoid alerting guards
- Herding mechanics to move cattle quietly
- Guard dogs that can detect intruders based on scent

### 4. Transportation Challenge
- Cattle moving speed and behavior varies by breed and conditions
- Weather affects movement rates and detection chances
- Roadblocks and checkpoints by law enforcement
- River crossings with risk of losing animals
- Tracking system for ranch owners to follow stolen cattle

### 5. Fencing Operation
- Multiple buyers with varying prices and risk levels
- Cattle branding system that requires modification
- Holding pens with maintenance requirements
- Reputation impacts with criminal and legitimate businesses
- Increasing risk of lawmen raids based on frequency

### 6. Consequences & Countermeasures
- Ranch owners receive alerts when rustling detected
- Posse formation for recovery operations
- Insurance system for ranch owners
- Bounties automatically placed on identified rustlers
- Ranch security upgrades available after incidents
- Town effects based on rustling frequency in region
