# RedM Roleplay Server - Jobs System

## Overview
A comprehensive jobs system offering various career paths, skill progression, and economic opportunities. Each job features unique mechanics, progression paths, specialized equipment, and integration with other server systems.

## Available Jobs

### 1. Lawman (Sheriff/Deputy)
- **Responsibilities:**
  - Patrolling towns and responding to crimes
  - Arresting and transporting criminals
  - Investigating crime scenes
  - Escorting valuable transports
  - Managing prison facilities
- **Progression:**
  - Deputy → Sheriff's Deputy → Sheriff → U.S. Marshal
  - Access to better weapons and equipment with rank
  - Command capabilities over lower-ranking officers
- **Special Mechanics:**
  - Crime scene investigation mini-game
  - Wanted poster creation and bounty setting
  - Access to law enforcement records
  - Authority to commandeer horses/wagons
  - Ability to call in NPC reinforcements

### 2. Doctor/Medicine Man
- **Responsibilities:**
  - Treating wounded players
  - Creating medicines from gathered herbs
  - Performing surgeries to heal serious injuries
  - Managing disease outbreaks in towns
  - Conducting medical research
- **Progression:**
  - Field Medic → Town Doctor → Surgeon → Chief Physician
  - Increased treatment effectiveness with rank
  - Ability to create more advanced medicines
- **Special Mechanics:**
  - Surgery mini-game for critical injuries
  - Herb identification and gathering system
  - Disease diagnosis challenge
  - Mobile clinic setup for remote operations
  - Patient record-keeping system

### 3. Hunter/Trapper
- **Responsibilities:**
  - Tracking and hunting wildlife
  - Skinning animals for pelts and materials
  - Setting traps for passive hunting
  - Crafting items from animal parts
  - Controlling predator populations near towns
- **Progression:**
  - Novice Hunter → Experienced Trapper → Master Hunter → Legendary Outdoorsman
  - Access to better tracking abilities and hunting grounds
  - More efficient skinning with less material damage
- **Special Mechanics:**
  - Hunting tracking system with visual clues
  - Weather and time effects on animal behaviors
  - Trophy system for rare/perfect kills
  - Trap placement and checking mechanic
  - Specialized ammo crafting for clean kills

### 4. Moonshiner/Distiller
- **Responsibilities:**
  - Gathering ingredients for spirits
  - Operating and maintaining a still
  - Producing quality moonshine
  - Distributing products while avoiding law enforcement
  - Developing signature recipes
- **Progression:**
  - Amateur Brewer → Established Distiller → Master Moonshiner → Liquor Baron
  - Improved recipe quality and production efficiency
  - Larger distribution network
- **Special Mechanics:**
  - Distilling mini-game affecting product quality
  - Hiding stills from law enforcement
  - Temperature and ingredient management
  - Risk/reward delivery system
  - Specialized recipe development

### 5. Blacksmith/Gunsmith
- **Responsibilities:**
  - Crafting and repairing weapons
  - Shoeing horses for improved performance
  - Creating tools for other professions
  - Forging decorative metal items
  - Upgrading existing equipment
- **Progression:**
  - Apprentice → Journeyman → Master Smith → Legendary Craftsman
  - Access to more complex blueprints
  - Higher quality results with better durability
- **Special Mechanics:**
  - Forging mini-game affecting item quality
  - Material sourcing and quality management
  - Custom weapon design system
  - Heat management during crafting
  - Blueprint discovery and research

### 6. Rancher/Farmer
- **Responsibilities:**
  - Growing crops with seasonal considerations
  - Raising and breeding livestock
  - Harvesting and selling produce
  - Maintaining irrigation systems
  - Protecting farm from weather and raiders
- **Progression:**
  - Farmhand → Small Farmer → Established Rancher → Agricultural Magnate
  - Access to better seeds and breeding stock
  - Larger land holdings and more efficient operations
- **Special Mechanics:**
  - Weather and seasonal effects on crops
  - Livestock breeding system for better animals
  - Irrigation and soil management
  - Market price fluctuations
  - Farm defense events against rustlers

### 7. Saloon Owner/Bartender
- **Responsibilities:**
  - Managing establishment inventory
  - Serving customers and maintaining order
  - Hiring entertainment (musicians, card dealers)
  - Gathering information from patrons
  - Hosting special events (poker tournaments, etc.)
- **Progression:**
  - Barkeep → Saloon Manager → Owner → Hospitality Tycoon
  - Larger, more prestigious establishments
  - Better profit margins and patron loyalty
- **Special Mechanics:**
  - Drink mixing mini-game for customer satisfaction
  - Establishment reputation management
  - Information broker system (overhearing valuable info)
  - Special event organization
  - Dealing with rowdy customers or troublemakers

### 8. Prospector/Miner
- **Responsibilities:**
  - Searching for valuable mineral deposits
  - Mining ore from discovered locations
  - Processing raw materials into usable resources
  - Maintaining mining equipment
  - Defending claims from jumpers
- **Progression:**
  - Novice Prospector → Experienced Miner → Mining Expert → Resources Baron
  - Access to better mining techniques and equipment
  - Ability to identify richer deposits
- **Special Mechanics:**
  - Prospecting system to locate resources
  - Mining mini-game for resource extraction
  - Claim staking and maintenance
  - Mine shaft construction and safety
  - Processing raw materials into sellable goods

### 9. Outlaw/Bandit
- **Responsibilities:**
  - Planning and executing robberies
  - Managing hideouts and gang members
  - Evading law enforcement
  - Intimidating targets for protection money
  - Building criminal reputation
- **Progression:**
  - Common Thief → Gang Member → Lieutenant → Gang Leader
  - Access to better criminal contacts and opportunities
  - Command over more gang members
- **Special Mechanics:**
  - Stealth and intimidation systems
  - Hideout management and defense
  - Fence system for stolen goods
  - Bounty and wanted level management
  - Territory control against rival gangs

### 10. Trader/Merchant
- **Responsibilities:**
  - Purchasing goods at low prices
  - Transporting merchandise between locations
  - Selling at profit in high-demand areas
  - Managing inventory and market knowledge
  - Establishing trade routes
- **Progression:**
  - Peddler → Shopkeeper → Merchant → Trade Magnate
  - Access to better suppliers and exclusive goods
  - Larger inventory capacity and trade network
- **Special Mechanics:**
  - Dynamic economy with regional price differences
  - Supply convoy management and protection
  - Market forecasting system
  - Store ownership and management
  - Special order fulfillment for premium customers

### 11. Bounty Hunter
- **Responsibilities:**
  - Tracking wanted criminals across the map
  - Capturing or eliminating targets based on bounty requirements
  - Gathering intelligence on target whereabouts
  - Transporting captured bounties to authorities
  - Negotiating bounty rewards
- **Progression:**
  - Amateur Tracker → Professional Hunter → Elite Bounty Hunter → Legendary Manhunter
  - Access to higher-tier bounties with better rewards
  - Enhanced tracking abilities and capture tools
- **Special Mechanics:**
  - Advanced tracking system with environmental clues
  - Bounty board with procedurally generated targets
  - Dead or alive capture options with different rewards
  - Reputation system affecting bounty availability
  - Trophy collection from notorious captures

### 12. Gambler/Card Shark
- **Responsibilities:**
  - Participating in various gambling activities
  - Hosting private games for high-rollers
  - Learning/mastering different card games and techniques
  - Building reputation in gambling circuits
  - Managing bankroll and calculating odds
- **Progression:**
  - Amateur Player → Regular Gambler → Professional Card Player → Gambling Legend
  - Access to higher-stakes games
  - Ability to read opponents better (subtle UI hints)
- **Special Mechanics:**
  - Card game skill mini-games
  - Cheating mechanics with risk/reward
  - Tournament organization and participation
  - Underground gambling circuit with exclusive access
  - Poker face/tells system affecting gameplay

- **Responsibilities:**

  - Negotiating with landowners for drilling rights
  - Transporting crude oil to refineries
- **Progression:**

  - More efficient extraction and refining techniques

  - Company management and expansion

  - Creating coffins and funerary items

  - Taking portraits for clients

  - Creating photo albums and collections

- **Progression:**
  - Amateur → Professional → Renowned → Legendary Photographer

  - News distribution network
  - Intelligence gathering from message content

  - Managing passenger and cargo transport

## Technical Requirements
- Skill progression tracking for each job
- Job-specific inventory and tool systems
- Reputation system with various factions
- Dynamic job markets with supply/demand mechanics
- Job certification and licensing system
- Multi-job compatibility for character specialization
- Job-specific animations and interactions
