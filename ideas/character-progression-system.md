# Character Progression & Identity System

## Overview
A comprehensive character development system that allows players to create immersive identities with meaningful progression over time. This system tracks physical attributes, skills, reputation, and personal history to create truly unique characters within the RedM world.

## Core Features

### 1. Character Creation
- **Physical Appearance**
  - Detailed character customization (age, build, facial features)
  - Period-appropriate clothing options
  - Scars, birthmarks, and distinguishing features
  - Voice selection affecting interaction responses

- **Background Selection**
  - Origin story choices affecting starting abilities
  - Family history options with potential NPC connections
  - Previous profession granting initial skill bonuses
  - Cultural background influencing dialect and interactions

- **Starting Resources**
  - Variable starting money based on background
  - Initial equipment packages tied to life path
  - Property deed options for established characters
  - Family heirlooms with unique properties

### 2. Attribute System
- **Physical Attributes**
  - Strength - affects carrying capacity, melee damage
  - Endurance - determines stamina and health regeneration
  - Dexterity - influences precision tasks and reload speed
  - Constitution - impacts resistance to environment and disease

- **Mental Attributes**
  - Intelligence - affects crafting quality and dialogue options
  - Perception - enhances tracking and environmental awareness
  - Charisma - influences prices and NPC interactions
  - Willpower - determines resistance to stress and addiction

- **Dynamic Attribute Changes**
  - Weight fluctuation based on diet
  - Muscle development from physical activities
  - Aging effects over extended play
  - Temporary buffs/debuffs from lifestyle choices

### 3. Skill Progression
- **Skill Categories**
  - Combat (shooting, melee, horseback combat)
  - Survival (hunting, fishing, foraging, cooking)
  - Crafting (woodworking, metalworking, leatherworking)
  - Social (persuasion, intimidation, bartering, leadership)
  - Knowledge (medicine, languages, history, science)

- **Experience System**
  - Skill-use-based progression (skills improve as used)
  - Challenges and milestones for skill advancement
  - Diminishing returns to prevent instant mastery
  - Skill decay for long-unused abilities

- **Learning Methods**
  - Self-practice with gradual improvement
  - Book reading for knowledge skills
  - NPC mentors offering specialized training
  - Group learning with faster progression

### 4. Health & Wellbeing
- **Injury System**
  - Locational damage with specific effects
  - Persistent injuries requiring medical treatment
  - Scar formation from healed wounds
  - Chronic conditions from untreated injuries

- **Needs Management**
  - Hunger affecting stamina and health regeneration
  - Thirst impacting physical performance
  - Rest requirements preventing continuous activity
  - Cleanliness affecting social interactions

- **Disease & Treatment**
  - Period-accurate illnesses with symptoms
  - Contagion mechanics for spread between players
  - Herbal and medical treatments
  - Recovery time based on care quality

### 5. Reputation & Social Standing
- **Faction Reputation**
  - Standing with law enforcement
  - Relationship with criminal organizations
  - Reputation in different towns and regions
  - Faction-specific opportunities and restrictions

- **Personal Fame/Infamy**
  - Recognition based on notable actions
  - Newspaper coverage of significant deeds
  - NPC reactions based on reputation
  - Wanted level affecting law enforcement response

- **Honor System**
  - Moral choices affecting character disposition
  - Visual cues reflecting honor level
  - NPC trust based on honorable actions
  - Special dialogue options based on honor

### 6. Psychological Elements
- **Stress & Trauma**
  - Stress accumulation from intense experiences
  - Trauma from witnessing or experiencing violence
  - Coping mechanisms (alcohol, gambling, etc.)
  - Recovery through rest, socialization, or therapy

- **Addiction System**
  - Dependence development from substance use
  - Withdrawal symptoms affecting gameplay
  - Tolerance increasing required dosage
  - Recovery path with relapse mechanics

- **Personality Development**
  - Trait acquisition based on repeated behaviors
  - Character quirks developing over time
  - Changing dialogue options reflecting personality
  - NPC compatibility based on personality traits

### 7. Lifestyle & Identity
- **Living Arrangements**
  - Housing options from camps to mansions
  - Home customization and upgrades
  - Practical benefits from quality housing
  - Rest quality based on accommodation

- **Personal Records**
  - Birth certificates and legal identity
  - Criminal records accessible to law enforcement
  - Medical history tracked by doctors
  - Property deeds and business licenses

- **Belongings & Inheritance**
  - Personal inventory aging and wear
  - Heirloom items with historical significance
  - Will system for character death
  - Legacy mechanics for new characters
