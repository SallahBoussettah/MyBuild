# Town Takeover & Territory Control

## Overview
Factions can attempt to seize control of smaller settlements through intimidation, economics, or force. Controlling territory provides benefits but requires maintenance and defense against rival factions and law enforcement.

## System Workflow

### 1. Faction Formation
- Minimum member requirement to form official faction
- Hierarchy system with leader, lieutenants, and members
- Faction base establishment with upgrade options
- Faction specialization (outlaws, vigilantes, businessmen)
- Uniform and insignia customization

### 2. Initial Influence Building
- Reputation quests with local businesses and citizens
- Business investment options in target town
- Property acquisition for faction operations
- Recruitment of NPC townsfolk as informants
- Local authorities relationship management

### 3. Territory Control Tactics
- Economic approach: business buyouts and price manipulation
- Intimidation approach: threatening businesses for protection money
- Force approach: direct confrontation with current controlling faction
- Political approach: bribing officials and manipulating elections
- Mixed strategies with different consequences

### 4. Takeover Operation
- Final push requires coordinated faction action
- Key location control (sheriff's office, saloon, general store)
- Civilian loyalty system influencing difficulty
- Opposition resistance scales with territory value
- Law enforcement response based on methods used

### 5. Territory Management
- Resource generation based on controlled businesses
- Security maintenance requirements
- Tax collection system from local operations
- Morale system affecting civilian cooperation
- Infrastructure improvements increasing territory value

### 6. Defending Territory
- Rival faction attack alerts
- Law enforcement raids based on criminal activity
- Defensive position establishment
- Loyalty missions to maintain civilian support
- Security patrol management
- Territory can decline if poorly managed
