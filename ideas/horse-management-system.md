# Horse Management & Stables System

## Overview
A comprehensive horse ownership and management system that makes horses more than just transportation. This system creates meaningful bonds between players and their mounts through care, training, and breeding mechanics while adding depth to the horse-centric Western experience.

## Horse Acquisition

### 1. Purchase Options
- **Stable Purchases**
  - Horse breed variety with different base stats
  - Age variations affecting potential and lifespan
  - Coat colors and markings for customization
  - Pre-trained options at premium prices
  
- **Auction System**
  - Scheduled horse auctions at major towns
  - Bidding mechanics against NPCs and players
  - Horse inspection period before purchase
  - History details for auctioned horses

- **Wild Horse Taming**
  - Regional wild horse spawns with unique traits
  - Tracking and approach mechanics
  - Breaking mini-game with difficulty by temperament
  - Bonding period after successful taming

### 2. Horse Characteristics
- **Breed Specializations**
  - War horses with combat advantages
  - Draft horses with cargo capacity
  - Race horses with speed advantages
  - Work horses with stamina benefits
  
- **Attribute System**
  - Speed - maximum velocity and acceleration
  - Stamina - endurance and recovery rate
  - Health - injury resistance and recovery
  - Handling - responsiveness and agility
  - Courage - behavior in combat and hazards
  - Intelligence - learning rate and memory

- **Visual Customization**
  - Grooming options affecting appearance
  - Mane and tail styling
  - Scarring and aging visual effects
  - Coat maintenance affecting glossiness
