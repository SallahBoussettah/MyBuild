# Bank Heist System

## Overview
A multi-stage bank robbery system centered around the Saint Denis bank. Players must plan, execute, and escape with the loot while evading law enforcement. The difficulty scales based on number of participants, time of day, and server conditions.

## System Workflow

### 1. Intelligence Gathering
- Players must first scout the bank during business hours
- Multiple entry points with varying difficulty (front door, back entrance, roof)
- Guard patrol schedules that change daily
- Ability to bribe bank employees for inside information
- Planning board available at outlaw camps

### 2. Equipment Preparation
- Specialized tools required (lockpicks, dynamite, safecracking tools)
- Disguises to reduce initial detection chance
- Getaway transportation planning (horses, wagons with variable speed/capacity)
- Optional weapon upgrades specific for the heist
- Limited-use heist equipment that must be crafted or purchased

### 3. Execution Phase
- Time-sensitive actions once heist begins
- Crowd control mechanics (intimidation system for NPCs)
- Vault access requires multiple players performing different roles
- Alarm systems that can be disabled but regenerate
- Safe cracking mini-game with skill-based success chance

### 4. Loot Management
- Variable cash and valuables distribution
- Weight system affecting movement speed
- Loot bags that can be dropped or stolen
- Special unique items with higher value but increased detection
- Risk/reward decisions on how much to take

### 5. Escape Sequence
- Heat level system that escalates over time
- Lawmen roadblocks that appear dynamically
- Escape route planning with multiple options
- Possibility to use river/train for escape
- Temporary safe houses to hide until heat diminishes

### 6. Post-Heist Consequences
- Bounties placed on participating criminals
- Bank closure period based on severity of heist
- Economic impact affecting regional prices
- Reputation changes with different factions
- Cooldown period before bank can be robbed again
- Laundering system required to use stolen money
