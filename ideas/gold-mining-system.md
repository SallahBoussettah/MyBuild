# Gold Mining & Claim Jumping

## Overview
A comprehensive mining system where players can stake claims, mine resources, and compete for valuable gold deposits. Claim jumping (stealing from others' mines) is possible but carries significant risks and consequences.

## System Workflow

### 1. Prospecting & Claim Registration
- Prospecting tool required to identify potential mining locations
- Soil sampling mini-game to determine resource quality
- Claim registration at government office in town
- Claim marker placement with visible boundaries
- Claim maintenance fees required weekly

### 2. Mine Development
- Initial investment in equipment (picks, shovels, dynamite)
- Mine entrance construction with varying security options
- Support beam mechanics to prevent cave-ins
- Water pump systems for flooded areas
- Worker hiring for increased production

### 3. Resource Extraction
- Mining mini-game based on skill and equipment quality
- Different techniques yield different results (panning, sluicing, blasting)
- Resource quality varies by location
- Fatigue system affecting mining efficiency
- Random events (cave-ins, equipment failure, rich strikes)

### 4. Claim Jumping Operations
- Scouting of target mine security and routines
- Trespassing detection based on noise and visibility
- Security bypass options (lockpicking, tunneling, bribery)
- Limited time window to extract resources before detection
- High-risk, high-reward balance

### 5. Security & Defense
- Claim owners can set traps and alarms
- Guard hiring with patrol schedules
- Security dogs to detect intruders
- Reinforced storage for processed gold
- Alert system for owner when claim is under attack

### 6. Resource Processing & Sale
- Raw ore requires processing before sale
- Processing facilities in towns with usage fees
- Transportation of processed gold carries robbery risk
- Multiple buyers with varying prices
- Banking options with security vs. carrying cash
- Tax system on legitimate sales vs. black market
