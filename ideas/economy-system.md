# RedM Roleplay Server - Economy System

## Overview
A dynamic and interconnected economy system that simulates realistic market forces in the Wild West era. This system ties together all server activities, creating meaningful economic decisions, regional variations, and player-driven market fluctuations.

## Core Economic Principles

### 1. Regional Resource Distribution
- **Natural Resources**
  - Each region has unique native resources (gold in mountains, crops in plains, etc.)
  - Resource quality and quantity varies by location
  - Seasonal variation affects availability and prices
  - Depletion mechanics for over-harvested areas
  
- **Production Facilities**
  - Region-specific production buildings (lumber mills, refineries, etc.)
  - Different efficiency rates based on proximity to resources
  - Upgrade paths requiring community investment
  - Specialized processing unique to certain towns

### 2. Dynamic Supply & Demand
- **Price Fluctuation System**
  - Base prices for all goods adjusted automatically
  - Prices increase as local supply decreases
  - Regional scarcity creates profitable trade opportunities
  - Price caps to prevent extreme market manipulation
  
- **Market Trends**
  - Regular market shifts creating temporary opportunities
  - Long-term economic cycles (booms and recessions)
  - News system announcing significant price changes
  - Historical price tracking available in banks/trading posts

### 3. Currency & Banking
- **Multi-Currency System**
  - Gold/silver coins as primary currency
  - Bank notes for larger transactions
  - Foreign currency with exchange rates
  - Collectible currency with numismatic value
  
- **Banking Services**
  - Personal accounts with deposit insurance
  - Interest accrual on saved funds
  - Loans with collateral requirements
  - Inter-bank transfers between major towns

- **Investment Opportunities**
  - Business partnerships with NPCs
  - Property investment with passive income
  - Stock certificates in major companies
  - Government bonds with scheduled returns

### 4. Transportation & Trade Routes
- **Cargo System**
  - Weight and volume limitations on player inventory
  - Wagons/trains/boats with varied capacity
  - Specialized containers for different goods (refrigerated, secure)
  - Loading/unloading time based on cargo type
  
- **Trade Route Development**
  - Established routes with reduced bandit activity
  - Opportunity for escort services and protection rackets
  - Route efficiency improvements through community projects
  - Weather and terrain effects on transportation

- **Import/Export Mechanics**
  - Foreign markets accessible at ports/borders
  - Specialty goods unavailable domestically
  - Customs duties and tariffs on certain goods
  - Smuggling mechanics for avoiding trade taxes

### 5. Business Ownership
- **Property Acquisition**
  - Purchasable business properties in all towns
  - Location affects customer traffic and sales
  - Property taxes based on value and location
  - Maintenance requirements to prevent degradation
  
- **Employee Management**
  - NPC hiring for basic operations
  - Player employment with contract system
  - Wage negotiation and performance bonuses
  - Staff training to improve efficiency

- **Business Operations**
  - Inventory management system
  - Operating hours configuration
  - Service quality affecting reputation
  - Competition mechanics with nearby businesses

### 6. Black Market Economy
- **Illegal Goods**
  - Contraband items with high-risk/high-reward trade
  - Specialized dealers in hidden locations
  - Price premium on illegal goods
  - Confiscation risk from law enforcement
  
- **Money Laundering**
  - Converting "dirty money" from crimes to legitimate funds
  - Front businesses to explain sudden wealth
  - Laundering fees based on amount and speed
  - Risk of investigation by lawmen

- **Fencing Operations**
  - Selling stolen goods through specialized dealers
  - Item identification/tracking system
  - Cooling off period for high-profile stolen items
  - Reputation system affecting fence prices

## Specialized Economic Systems

### 1. Livestock Economy
- **Breeding & Raising**
  - Animal growth cycles requiring investment
  - Feed costs varying by season and region
  - Disease management affecting herd value
  - Genetic traits affecting animal value
  
- **Trading & Auctions**
  - Livestock auctions held on schedule
  - Bidding system for prized animals
  - Transport costs based on distance
  - Reputation system affecting prices offered

### 2. Mining Economy
- **Claim Value System**
  - Ore quality varying by location
  - Claim productivity declining over time
  - Prospecting tools to evaluate potential value
  - Claim size affecting maximum yield
  
- **Refining & Processing**
  - Raw ore to refined metal conversion
  - Processing costs at refineries
  - Quality grading affecting final value
  - Specialized uses for different metals

### 3. Agricultural Economy
- **Crop Management**
  - Seasonal planting cycles
  - Weather effects on crop yield
  - Irrigation and fertilization mechanics
  - Pest/disease management
  
- **Market Delivery**
  - Freshness timer on harvested crops
  - Preservation methods extending shelf life
  - Bulk sales to processing facilities
  - Direct-to-consumer premium pricing

### 4. Crafting Economy
- **Material Sourcing**
  - Raw material quality tiers
  - Material origin affecting properties
  - Rare component discovery system
  - Sustainable harvesting mechanics
  
- **Production Chain**
  - Multi-stage crafting for complex items
  - Tool quality affecting output quality
  - Workshop upgrades improving efficiency
  - Batch production for bulk orders

### 5. Service Economy
- **Skill-Based Pricing**
  - Service quality tied to skill level
  - Reputation affecting service demand
  - Specialized services commanding premium prices
  - Competition affecting market rates
  
- **Service Businesses**
  - Appointment scheduling system
  - Regular client bonuses
  - Service packages with discounts
  - Mobile services with travel fees
